Discovery of Roman-Era Shipwrecks in Abu Qir Bay – November 2017

Location: Abu Qir Bay, Alexandria, Egypt

In November 2017, archaeologists uncovered three Roman-era shipwrecks in Abu Qir Bay. Among the artifacts recovered were a crystal sculpture head, three gold coins from the era of Emperor Octavius Augustus, large wooden planks, and pottery vessels. Significance: These findings shed light on the maritime activities and trade networks of ancient Alexandria, offering a glimpse into the city's historical significance as a bustling port during the Roman period.
