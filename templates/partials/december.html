Discovery of the World's Oldest Known Marine Plant - December 2024

Location: Baltic Sea

Scientists identified a clone of Zostera marina (eelgrass) estimated to be 1,402 years old, making it the oldest known marine plant. Using a genetic clock calibrated with a 17-year-old lab-grown clone, researchers compared genetic variations to determine the age of the wild sample. This discovery highlights the longevity and resilience of seagrass meadows, which play a crucial role in carbon sequestration and providing habitat for marine life.
