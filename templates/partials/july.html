Discovery of the Ruby Seadragon in the Wild - July 30, 2015

Location: Off the remote coast of Western Australia

Scientists from the University of Western Australia and Scripps Institution of Oceanography observed the Ruby Seadragon in its natural habitat for the first time. The Ruby Seadragon, first identified from preserved specimens in 2015, is a deep-red relative of the more famous leafy and weedy seadragons. The live footage captured in July 2015 confirmed that this species lives in deeper, darker waters (50+ meters), which explains its camouflaging red coloration. It marked the first new seadragon species discovery in over 150 years.
